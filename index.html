ABOUT OUR PROJECT

One Platform. One Connected Shopping Experience.

SmartMall is a smart solution designed to improve the shopping experience inside malls.

Users can manage their shopping lists, share them with others, track their purchases, and keep a complete record of their shopping activity.

Couriers can receive assigned shopping tasks, view required items, and update their progress.

Mall security can verify users and monitor activity, creating a more organized and connected environment.

Connected System Shopper → Courier → Security

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SmartMall.

What is SmartMall?

SmartMall is a smart mall platform that connects shoppers, couriers, and security in one system.

What can users do with the platform?

Users can create shopping lists, share them with others, track shopping activity, and view purchase history.

Can I share my shopping list?

Yes. Users can share their shopping lists with another person so they can shop for the required products.

What can couriers do?

Couriers can view assigned shopping tasks, see the required products, and update task progress.

How does security work?

The platform provides tools for user verification and monitoring relevant mall activity.

Can I view my previous purchases?

Yes. Completed purchases can be saved so users can review their shopping history later.
